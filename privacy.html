Privacy Policy

Frank Fitness Pipeline · Last updated November 10, 2026

Frank Fitness Pipeline (including the Frank Fitness Editor) is a private tool built and used only by Owen Frank (owenfrank000@gmail.com) to manage the content for his own fitness business. It is not available to the public and does not let anyone else log in.

What it accesses

Instagram (Owen's own professional account only): Owen's posts and their performance statistics, and the direct messages sent to Owen's account. It does not access any other account.

Google Drive (Owen's own Drive only): raw video clips Owen uploads to a designated folder, and the finished, edited videos saved back to that Drive.

How the data is used

Post statistics are used to understand which of Owen's videos perform well. Direct messages are read only to identify people who ask Owen about his coaching, so he can follow up with them; the tool keeps the minimum needed for that (such as the date and the sender's Instagram handle), not full conversations. Video files are processed temporarily in an automated, private job and deleted from that job when it finishes. Results are stored in Owen's private code repository.

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

If you have sent a message to Owen's Instagram account and want any record of it removed from this tool, email owenfrank000@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your Instagram handle. Any stored record will be deleted within 30 days and you will get a confirmation by email.

Removing access

Owen can revoke the tool's access at any time: for Instagram, in Instagram's settings under apps and websites; for Google, in the Google Account's Security settings under third-party connections.
